    Get ready for the first-ever Counter-Strike 2 Major in Southeast Asia! 
     
    The most prestigious Counter-Strike 2 trophy is officially up for grabs, as the PGL CS2 Major Singapore welcomes us to an intense, four-day live-action finale.
